How a consortium works

One shared pool. Random selection, done right.

A drug testing consortium combines drivers from many carriers into a single random testing pool. It lets owner-operators and smaller fleets take part in a compliant random testing program without running one on their own.

  • Enrollment supportWe enroll drivers and companies into approved consortium programs.
  • Random testing coordinationSelections managed and testing schedules followed.
  • Compliance trackingProgram records organized for drivers and your company.
  • Ongoing assistanceContinued support as requirements evolve.
