What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in much the same way. They let a site remember information between pages and visits, for example the choices you made in a cookie banner.

Why this site uses them

We keep storage to a minimum. This site uses strictly necessary storage to work properly and to remember your consent, and, only if you agree, functional storage to remember preferences such as the services you selected. No non-essential storage is used until you give consent. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Cookie categories

Strictly necessary Always on

Needed for the site to work. Covers security, spam protection on forms, session handling and remembering your cookie choice. These cannot be switched off in Cookie Settings.

Functional Your choice

Remembers your preferences on this device, such as the services you selected on the order form or the documents you selected on the compliance page. Off unless you turn it on.

Analytics and marketing Not used

This site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If we ever add them, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before they load.

Third-party services

  • Analytics: none in use.
  • Spam protection and form delivery: FormSubmit, listed above.
  • Embedded maps or videos: none. The "Open in Maps" link on our contact page opens Google Maps in a new tab, where Google's own policies apply.
  • Customer Portal: the portal at account.tribrothersusa.com uses the storage it needs for sign-in and security.
  • Social links: links to Facebook, Instagram and TikTok open those sites, which apply their own cookie policies. No social media scripts run on this site.

Managing or deleting cookies

You can review, change or withdraw your consent at any time by selecting Cookie Settings, which appears in the footer of every page. Withdrawing consent removes functional storage from your device.

You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser. These guides explain how in common browsers:

Google Chrome Apple Safari Mozilla Firefox Microsoft Edge

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to keep non-essential storage off, and the cookie banner tells you that the signal was detected.

Questions

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, contact us. See also our Privacy Policy.
