Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the www.tribrothersusa.com website and governs data collection and usage. For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, unless otherwise noted, all references to Tri Brothers LLC include www.tribrothersusa.com. The Tri Brothers website is a trucking services platform. By using the Tri Brothers website, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

What is personal data?

"Personal Data" is any information that can reasonably be used to identify an individual or otherwise make an individual identifiable. This may include your name, address, email address, phone number, login information (such as account number and password) or payment card number.

The types of Personal Data we may process depend on the business context and purposes for which it was collected. It may include:

  • Contact, subscription and account or registration details, as well as online identifiers.
  • Login credentials where an account is required.
  • Communications content when you interact with Tri Brothers.
  • Financial information, such as invoices and payment details.
  • Details related to your business or service requirements.
  • System usage information such as device identifiers or IP addresses.

If we link other data with your Personal Data, we will treat that linked data as Personal Data.

Information we collect

Tri Brothers LLC may collect personally identifiable information, such as your name, phone number and email address. When you use a form on this website, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, company, USDOT number, the services you select and your message.

Additional information may be gathered automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, domain names, access times and referring website addresses. This data is used to operate the service and maintain its quality and security.

How we use your information

Tri Brothers LLC collects and uses your personal information to:

  • Operate its website and deliver the services you request.
  • Respond to inquiries and service requests you send through our forms.
  • Provide a secure and optimized user experience.
  • Communicate important updates and, where you have agreed, promotional offers.

We may also collect data from trusted third-party sources and use it to improve our services.

Sharing your information

Tri Brothers LLC does not sell, rent or lease customer lists to third parties. We may share data with trusted partners for specific purposes, such as customer support, while maintaining confidentiality.

We use service providers to run this website. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by a form delivery service, and web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These providers are listed in our Cookie Policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party websites you choose to visit from www.tribrothersusa.com. Tri Brothers is not responsible for the privacy policies or content of external sites.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses strictly necessary storage to run the site and remember your choices. With your consent, it also uses functional storage to remember preferences such as the services you selected. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our Cookie Policy lists every cookie and similar technology the site uses.

You can review, change or withdraw your consent at any time using Cookie Settings, which also appears in the footer of every page. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work properly if necessary storage is blocked.

Your privacy rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. To start this process, contact us at info@tribrothersusa.com.

Depending on where you live, including California under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and the European Economic Area or United Kingdom under the GDPR, you may also have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, such as consent to functional storage.

We honor Global Privacy Control signals sent by your browser as a request to keep non-essential storage off. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Security of your personal information

Tri Brothers secures your data against unauthorized access, use or disclosure through robust security measures.

Children under thirteen

Tri Brothers LLC does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under thirteen. If you are under thirteen, you must obtain permission from a parent or guardian to use this website.
